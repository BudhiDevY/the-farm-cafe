# The Farm Cafe — Staff & Owner Operations Manual

Welcome to **The Farm Cafe** digital ordering and table reservation system.

## 1. Important Links
- **Customer Menu & Ordering:** `https://your-domain.com/`
- **Staff & Owner Portal:** `https://your-domain.com/admin.html`
- **Default Admin PIN:** `2027` *(Change this immediately after first login)*

---

## 2. Managing Live Orders (`Counter` & `Chef / Kitchen`)
1. Open `admin.html` on the counter tablet/laptop and enter your **Staff PIN**.
2. **Counter Tab:**
   - New customer orders (`Dine-in`, `Kerbside Pickup`, or `Delivery`) appear here automatically.
   - Click **Send to Chef** to forward the ticket to the kitchen screen.
   - *(Tip: Uncheck **"Forward orders from Counter to Chef/Kitchen"** at the top if a single person handles both counter and kitchen).*
3. **Chef / Kitchen Tab:**
   - Displays active food tickets with item quantities and table/pickup details.
   - Click **Mark Ready** once the food is prepared.
4. **Ready to Serve & Auto-Clear:**
   - Ready orders appear in the green banner at the top of the **Counter** tab.
   - Click **Served & Clear** once handed to the customer, or let the system clear it automatically after **2 minutes**.

---

## 3. Managing the Menu (`Menu Manager`)
- The 26 signature dishes remain built-in on the customer menu.
- **Add a New Dish:**
  1. Go to **Menu Manager** → **+ Add New Item**.
  2. Enter the Dish Name, Category (`Small plates`, `Continental`, `Oriental`, or `Farm cafe special`), Price (`₹`), Dietary Type (`Veg` / `Non-Veg`), optional Tag, and Description.
  3. Click **Add Dish**—it appears live on the customer menu immediately.
- **Remove a Custom Dish:** Click **Delete** next to any custom item in **Menu Manager**.

---

## 4. Managing Table Reservations (`Bookings`)
- Go to the **Bookings** tab to view guest names, phone numbers, party sizes, and requested dates/times.
- Call the guest to confirm, and click **Delete** after their visit to keep the list tidy.

---

## 5. Changing Your Staff PIN
1. Click **Change PIN** in the top navigation bar of `admin.html`.
2. Enter your **Current PIN** and your **New PIN** (4 or more digits).
3. Click **Save PIN**—the new PIN syncs immediately across all staff devices.
